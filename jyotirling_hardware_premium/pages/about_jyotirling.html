Our Story

Founded in 2024 by Aniket Sutar, our company began with a simple yet profound vision: to transform the modern hardware supply experience by bringing the concept of ‘divine light’ to every interaction, every product, and every customer relationship.

What began in 2024 as a simple door hardware store in Palus, Maharashtra, is steadily becoming a trusted name for quality and reliability in the local community. Our name, Jyotirling, reflects the guiding light we bring to every home and every customer—helping them find the right hardware solutions with care and sincerity.

Today, we stand as more than a supplier; we are curators of quality, advisors in excellence, and partners in your success. Every product in our catalog is selected not just for functionality, but for durability, innovation, and aesthetic appeal.
