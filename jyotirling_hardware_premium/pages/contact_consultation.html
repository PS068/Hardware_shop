Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about our services and consultation process

How much does a consultation cost?

Project planning consultations are complimentary for professional contractors and trade accounts. For homeowners, we offer free product selection guidance. Technical support sessions are ₹500, which is fully refundable with any purchase over ₹5,000.

Can I get consultation for projects outside Mumbai?

Yes! We offer virtual consultations for clients across India. Our specialists can provide detailed guidance via video calls, and we can arrange product samples and technical documentation to be shipped to your location.

What should I prepare for my consultation?

Bring project drawings, photos of existing conditions, measurements, and your budget range. For renovation projects, photos of current hardware and any specific requirements help our specialists provide targeted recommendations.

Do you provide installation services?

We partner with certified installers across major cities. During consultation, we can connect you with trusted professionals in your area and provide detailed installation specifications for your chosen hardware.
